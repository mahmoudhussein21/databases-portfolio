I learned that a database needs to be organized properly because small mistakes can cause bigger problems. For example, a wrong machine number or a duplicated part can lead to confusion and incorrect information.

I liked discovering the five problems in the database. I understood why having the same part twice is a problem and why prices should be stored as numbers instead of text. I also learned that storing two parts together makes them harder to find and count.
